Dashed cells are editable assumptions — every gold value recalculates live
Formula parity with the workbook. Revenuet = Revenuet−1 × (1 + growtht) · Net Incomet = NIt−1 × (1 + NI growtht) · Margin = NI ÷ Revenue · EPS2026 is the reported figure from Company Inputs, EPSt>2026 = NIt ÷ shares · Share Price = EPS × P/E · CAGR = (final ÷ initial)1/4 − 1. Zero-denominator cases resolve to 0, matching the workbook's IFERROR wrappers.

Revenue and Net Income are in $ millions; EPS and share prices in $ per share. The workbook leaves 2028–2030 growth and P/E blank as ENTER VALUES placeholders; this app carries the prior year's assumption forward so the projection is never dead, and every carried cell is editable above. Share counts are held flat across all five years, as in the workbook.
